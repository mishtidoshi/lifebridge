Renew Every Year

Each year, your county will send you a letter about your Medi‑Cal. The letter will tell you if you need to do anything.

Auto Renewal Letter

We already have your information. Your Medi‑Cal will stay active. You don’t need to do anything. You can keep using your benefits.

Renewal Form

We need a little more information from you. Fill out the renewal form and send it back quickly to keep your Medi‑Cal.
