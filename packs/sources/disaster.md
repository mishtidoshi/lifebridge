## Check by hand before submitting

1. **FEMA deadlines differ for each disaster**, so the pack doesn't list one. It asks the user to look up
   their disaster's deadline on DisasterAssistance.gov.
2. **No state-specific steps yet** (for example, CAL FIRE or state emergency offices). Every step is
   federal and works in any state.
3. Re-check FEMA's pages close to submission, since FEMA programs and phone hours can change.
