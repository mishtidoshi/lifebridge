## Check by hand before submitting

1. **Rules are still changing.** The federal work rule is an interim final rule and may change, states
   can ask for more time, and court cases are possible. Re-check the Federal Register page and DHCS
   "Medi-Cal Changes" before recording the demo.
2. **Other states get general steps.** Only California has state-specific steps (BenefitsCal, 10-day
   reporting, 90-day cure period, State Fair Hearing). Other states point to their state Medicaid
   agency. Six-month renewals are only listed for California, because no federal plain-language page
   was found to cite for other states.
3. **Non-expansion states.** The work rules only apply to adults who got Medicaid through the
   Affordable Care Act expansion. In states that didn't expand Medicaid, few adults will be affected;
   the step says the Medicaid office will send a letter if the rules apply.
4. Ask someone who helps families enroll (a county worker, clinic, or enrollment assister) to skim the
   California steps.
